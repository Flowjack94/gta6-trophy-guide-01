Roadmap to the Platinum

Four stages, in order. The story first, then the world, then clean-up, then 100%. The platinum pops when the other 54 are done.

  1. Stage 1 — Story first 28 trophies

    Play the campaign from Chapter 1 to the final mission. Every story trophy — the four chapter completions, the named missions, the character strands for Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy and Tyrone — pops as you go. Two habits matter here:

    • Do the missable first: meditate at Lucia's balcony spot in Chapter 1 for Positive Mental Attitude. It is the only trophy reported as possibly missable, and it disappears after Chapter 1.
    • Keep manual saves: one at the start of every chapter and one before the final mission. GTA VI has no mission replay, so these saves are your safety net.
    • Try every activity type once as you meet it — that is Master of None — and grab vehicles and WAiNK spots as you travel.

  2. Stage 2 — The open world exploration & strangers

    After the story, explore all of Leonida (The Goodtime State), finish the 8 stranger mission strands (Strangers & Freaks), hunt the unique animal for Bret (Fair Game), and work the long grinds while you travel: 100 vehicles discovered, 100 WAiNK locations, meditation spots, and collectible card sets.

  3. Stage 3 — Clean-up activities, robberies & money

    Now the dedicated grinds: 250 stunts, 20 different race wins, gold at every Shooting Range challenge, drone races at every location, 50 challenges, the robbery set (shops, jewelry store, armored truck, bank vaults, gang operations), and the money set — $250,000 laundered, $100,000 spent, all garages bought, 10 vehicles registered.

  4. Stage 4 — 100% completion the gold

    Finish A Long Road — 100% completion. The in-game stats screen shows your percentage and what is still missing. Check it regularly in this stage so nothing is left at 99%. When it pops, the platinum Vice Got a Price! pops with it.

Golden rules

  • Cheats disable trophies — don't use them on a platinum run.
  • Manual save at the start of every chapter + one before the final mission.
  • Do Lucia's balcony meditation in Chapter 1 — the one reported missable.
  • Grinds (stunts, WAiNK, vehicles) are best done passively while travelling, not in one sitting.
